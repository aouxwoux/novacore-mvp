THE MVP

Built to prove the workflow.

NOVACORE connects packed-parcel measurements to a versioned freight contract, then compares the expected charge with courier invoices.

This static demo stores records and uploaded evidence on this browser only. Sample shipments, contracts, invoices, and claim outcomes are illustrative. It does not connect to couriers, send claims, verify credits, or control a scale.

Only cases explicitly marked Credit confirmed contribute to the recovered amount. Packaging savings are estimates that require a damage-and-returns pilot.

For a pilot: connect a scale and barcode scanner, secure shared evidence storage, add real rate cards, and confirm credits against courier statements.
